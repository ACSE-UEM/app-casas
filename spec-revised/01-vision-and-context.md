# 1. Product Vision and Context

## 1.1. Product Vision
The Spiritist Center Management System is a national platform designed to unify, organize, and facilitate the discovery of Spiritist Centers (Casas Espíritas) and their activities across Brazil. The system serves as a centralized hub for institutional transparency, volunteer engagement, and public outreach, while respecting the autonomy and regional structures of the Spiritist Movement.

## 1.2. National Scope and Organizational Hierarchy
The system must be flexible enough to accommodate the structural variations across all Brazilian states (Federativas).

The general hierarchy is:
1. **Federativa (State Level):** The primary state organization.
2. **Macro-região (Macro-regional Level):** Regional groupings (e.g., Comissões Regionais in MG).
3. **Micro-região (Micro-regional Level):** Sub-regional groupings (e.g., Conselhos Espíritas Regionais in MG).
4. **Órgão Intermunicipal / Municipal:** Local alliances (e.g., Alianças Espíritas Municipais).
5. **Centro Espírita (Casa):** The autonomous institution itself.

*Note: The platform must support states where intermediate levels (Macro/Micro/Municipal) do not exist (e.g., Distrito Federal).*

## 1.3. Definition of a Spiritist Center (Casa Espírita)
A Centro Espírita is an autonomous spiritist organization serving the local community through ongoing activities and specific events. It is sustained by volunteers (tarefeiros) and managed by directors/coordinators.

## 1.4. Target Audience (Stakeholders)
The platform must cater to diverse audiences, prioritizing their needs in the following order (Service-oriented design):

1. **General Public / Society:** People seeking help, knowledge, or a place to visit (Fraternal Assistance, Lectures, Events).
2. **Volunteers / Tarefeiros:** Individuals looking to help, volunteer, or participate in specific activities.
3. **House Coordinators:** Volunteers managing specific tasks or departments within a Center.
4. **House Directors (Admin):** Leaders responsible for the Center's overall management and institutional presence.
5. **Federation Representatives (Macro/Micro/State levels):** Regional coordinators who seek to serve and support groups of Centers, requiring consolidated data and compliance overviews.
